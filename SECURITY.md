# Security policy

**Found a vulnerability? Report it privately, and we'll fix it before anyone else hears about it.**

</div>

1. **Report privately:** the repo's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, never a public issue, PR or discussion. Include what you found, how to reproduce it and the impact you expect.
2. **What happens next:** a reply within 5 working days; a fix within 30 days for a high or critical issue, or a plan with a date; then an advisory crediting you, unless you'd rather not be named.
3. **In scope:** the `main` branch: code, workflows, scripts and infrastructure as code.
4. **Out of scope:** risks accepted in [guides/03-security-trade-offs.md](guides/03-security-trade-offs.md), the starter's placeholder docs, and anything needing an already compromised GitHub or AWS account.
5. **Testing:** use your own copy, never someone else's deployment, and don't access or keep anyone's data. Good-faith research under this policy won't be pursued.

To contribute a fix, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). [MIT licence](LICENSE).
